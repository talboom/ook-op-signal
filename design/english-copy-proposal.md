### whySignal.whatIsSignal

**Old:**
> Signal is a privacy-focused messaging app that protects your communication with end-to-end encryption. Only you and the recipient can read the messages. Unlike many other apps, Signal collects no unnecessary data. It is open-source, so experts can review the code. By switching you choose a safer, independent alternative that respects your privacy.

**Proposed:**

## Why Signal page — benefits

### whySignal.benefit1Title / benefit1

**Old:**
> **Non-profit:** Signal is a foundation without shareholders or venture capitalists and is funded by donations instead of ads.

**Proposed:**
> **Non-profit:** No shareholders, no ads. Signal is funded by donations. This means they work for you, not advertisers.

### whySignal.benefit2Title / benefit2

**Old:**
> **Privacy by design:** Signal collects no user data and offers the highest level of privacy. Even WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger use Signal's encryption protocol.

**Proposed:**
> **Privacy by design:** Signal collects nothing about you. No personal data stored. Even WhatsApp uses Signal's encryption protocol — they just don't share Signal's values.

### whySignal.benefit3Title / benefit3

**Old:**
> **Ethical leadership:** CEO Meredith Whittaker champions ethical tech and fights Big Tech surveillance.

**Proposed:**
> **Ethical leadership:** CEO Meredith Whittaker is one of the loudest voices against Big Tech surveillance. 

### whySignal.benefit4Title / benefit4

**Old:**
> **Independent infrastructure:** Signal protects users worldwide without compromising privacy or usability.

**Proposed:**
> **Independent infrastructure:** Signal runs its own servers, independent from Big Tech. No compromises on privacy or usability.

### whySignal.openSourceTitle / openSourceText

**Old:**
> **Open Source and Transparency:** Signal is open-source, giving everyone the chance to review the code and verify there are no backdoors – building transparency and trust.

**Proposed:**
> **Open source and transparency:** Signal's code is public. Anyone can inspect it, verify there are no backdoors, and hold them accountable.
