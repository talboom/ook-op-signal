# Ook op Signal — Brand Identity & Design Direction

## Who we are

A grassroots movement, not a corporation. We're the friend who says "hey, you should really try this" — not the ad that tells you to. The tool is dead simple (upload, badge, download), and the design should match that energy: confident, clear, zero friction.

## Target audience

Privacy-conscious Europeans (20–45) who already *want* to switch to Signal but need their network to follow. They're digitally literate, share things on social media, and respond to design that feels contemporary — not clinical, not corporate, not "tech startup landing page #4,782."

## Tone of voice

**Friendly activist.** Direct, warm, slightly cheeky. We're not lecturing — we're rallying. Think "join the party" rather than "protect your data." The privacy argument is there for whoever wants it, but the front door is social proof and momentum.

## Look & feel

### Current state
The site works but reads as a functional prototype — Bulma defaults, system fonts, grey backgrounds, utilitarian layout. It doesn't *feel* like a movement you want to join.

### Where we're going

**Bold, saturated, alive.** Drawing from the inspiration references:

- **Donnie** — Unapologetic use of a single bold colour as the dominant surface. Big, punchy type. Playful but not childish. Cards with thick borders and shadows that feel tactile.
- **Clarify** — Organic, flowing shapes. Beautiful typography as a centrepiece. A sense of *space* and breathing room. Confident editorial quality.

### Design principles for the revamp

1. **Signal Blue owns the room.** Use Signal's blue (`#3b45fd`) as the dominant, hero-level colour — not as a timid accent. Think full-bleed blue sections, not a blue button on a white page. Pair with off-white/cream and a dark navy for contrast.

2. **Big, characterful typography.** Drop system fonts. Use a bold, slightly rounded display face (think Satoshi, General Sans, or Clash Display from Fontshare) for headings. Something that feels approachable but modern. Clean geometric sans for body text.

3. **Tactile, graphic elements.** Thick borders, visible drop shadows (like Donnie's card style), rounded corners. Things should feel like you can pick them up. The badge thumbnails should feel like stickers you're choosing from.

4. **Organic accents.** Subtle blob shapes or soft gradients in the background à la Clarify — keeps it from feeling too boxy. A gentle grain/noise texture overlay adds warmth and keeps it from looking "too digital."

5. **Minimal steps, maximum delight.** The three-step flow (upload → position → download) should feel like a smooth ride, not a form. Micro-animations on upload, a satisfying badge-select interaction, a celebratory download moment.

6. **European, multilingual, inclusive.** The language selector should be prominent and well-designed — it's a feature, not an afterthought. The 10-language support is a point of pride.

## Brands & references to draw from

| Reference | What to borrow |
|---|---|
| **Donnie** (doedonnie.nl) | Bold single-colour dominance, thick card borders, playful confidence |
| **Clarify** (clarify.nl) | Organic shapes, editorial typography, breathing room |
| **Signal itself** | The blue, the clean simplicity, the privacy-first messaging |
| **Nothing Phone / Teenage Engineering** | Graphic, tactile UI that feels physical. Bold without being loud |
| **Oatly** | Activist brand energy — making a cause feel fun and obvious |

## Colour palette (starting point)

| Role | Colour | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Primary | `#3b45fd` (Signal Blue) | Hero sections, primary buttons, dominant surface |
| Primary dark | `#1e2280` | Nav, footer, text on light backgrounds |
| Accent | `#60d67a` | Success states, download button, "go" moments |
| Surface | `#f5f0eb` | Warm off-white background — not cold grey |
| Surface alt | `#e8e0d6` | Cards, secondary surfaces |
| Text | `#0f1923` | Near-black, warm |
| Text muted | `#5a6a7a` | Captions, notes |

## Typography direction

- **Display / headings:** A bold, characterful sans-serif with personality. Rounded or slightly geometric. Loaded via Google Fonts or Fontshare for zero build-step overhead.
- **Body:** A clean, highly readable sans-serif that pairs well. Slightly warm.
- **The logo "Ook op Signal"** should feel hand-set, bold, uppercase — like a protest sign or bumper sticker.

## Summary

The revamp should make you *want* to add the badge. It should feel like joining something, not filling out a form. Bold blue, big type, tactile interactions, a warm-not-cold palette, and the confidence of a brand that knows exactly what it's about.
